The [ASINA project](https://www.asina-project.eu/) just published a paper about a "a collection of open FAIR datasets for titanium dioxide coated photocatalytic surfaces".
I described the FAIRification process of many datasets they releases in [this Zenodo collection](https://zenodo.org/communities/asina/records?q=&f=resource_type%3Adataset&l=list&p=4&s=10&sort=newest).
A fair number of these datasets use the ERM identifiers the project registered earlier. These are now linked
to the ERM identifiers in this database. They also make nice additions to the
[Overview of open datasets released by NanoSafety Cluster projects](https://nanocommons.github.io/datasets/).
